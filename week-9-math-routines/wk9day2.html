Week 9 · Second session — Studio

Act II · design the After · how will you capture what students' thinking leaves behind?
Today's goals: Design the After — the piece that captures students' thinking so you can analyze it later. Type it into the BDA Planner, show it to a classmate, then get the genAI planning partner's read. Human first, machine second.

1 · The After — the capture (~30 min)

  • Your During is where the thinking happens — but thinking evaporates. The After is how you capture it: a short artifact you physically collect, plus a question or two that pushes the reasoning. Skip it and you finish the lesson holding nothing to analyze.
  • The Planner offers four kinds of After. Pick the one a student's response could answer your research question with:
    • Exit slip — one to three prompts on paper at the end; you keep every response.
    • Discussion transcript — two or three questions you ask in the closing discussion; you record what students actually say.
    • Strategy gallery — chosen solutions go to the board in an order you set; the artifact is the annotated photo.
    • One-on-one probe — three questions for one focus student, one on one, with a jot-your-reasoning prompt.
  • Weak vs. strong, same task: "did everyone get it? thumbs up" leaves you a nodding room and nothing to read; "draw where 3/4 sits and write one sentence on how you decided" leaves you holding the reasoning. The test: would a student's response leave me holding evidence I could hold up against my research question? If it can be answered without revealing any thinking, it isn't a capture yet.

Purpose: the After is the most-skipped, least-planned piece — and the one that decides whether you walk out with data.

2 · Your turn — build your After (~35 min)

  • Develop (~20 min). Type your After into the BDA Planner — Plan → After: the artifact you'll collect and the question(s) that push the thinking.
  • A classmate looks (~15 min). Share your task and your research question. Then show your classmate your After: could a student finish it without showing any thinking? Then swap.

3 · The genAI planning partner (~15 min)

  • Press Check this on your After, then follow up with Ask me. Out of time? Do it at home.
  • The genAI planning partner is a critic, not a designer. Keep what is useful and Set aside what is not.
Wrap-up & looking ahead:
  • Before our next class, revise your After from both reads: your classmate's and the genAI planning partner's.
  • The Math Routine one-pager is due Sunday.
  • Next class, a classmate reads your whole lesson — Before, During, and After — against your research question.
