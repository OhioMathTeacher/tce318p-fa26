The DOK Wheel

Webb's Depth of Knowledge · not how hard the numbers are — how deep the thinking goes

Tap a wedge to see what that level asks for. Spin to get a level at random, then rewrite one of the five pentomino questions at that depth. Notice Compare, Construct and Critique each sit in two wedges, and the hub's three verbs live in all four: a verb alone never sets the level — what the learner has to do with it does.

Practice · which level is this question?

Each question is about pentominoes. Decide its depth, then see why.

Write your own

Write a pentomino question, pick the level you intended, and have a partner rate it before you say. If you disagree, that's the conversation. You can also copy a prompt and ask Gemini for a second opinion.
