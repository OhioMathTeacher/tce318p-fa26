Week 10 · First session — Review

Act II · test the whole lesson · would students' responses from this lesson provide evidence for your question? · your group of three reads the whole plan, then a genAI planning partner
Today's goals: In groups of three, read each other's whole plan against each research question — would running this actually tell you what you want to know? Then a genAI planning partner does the same. You revise from both reads next class. Human first, machine second.

1 · Housekeeping: share your plan (~10 min)
  • In the BDA Planner, go to Share → Feedback copy (Word). Upload it to your Google Drive and open it with Google Docs.
  • In that Google Doc: Share → General access → Anyone with the link · Commenter → Copy link. Then open this sheet (sign in with your Miami account). On your section's tab, Oxford or Hamilton, add your row: your name, your partner's name if you're sharing a plan, your research question, and the link.

2 · Review in groups of three (~45 min)
  • Open your plan in the BDA Planner — Plan → All three — and state your research question out loud. The question is the measuring stick: your group can't judge whether the lesson answers it until they know exactly what you're trying to find out.
  • Read each plan in turn — about 15 minutes per plan. Be concrete: point to where a student could finish without showing thinking, where the Before might give the move away, and whether the After's capture would hold up against their question.
  • Type comments to your classmate directly in their Google Doc. Refer to the peer-review sheet for guidance on feedback to provide.

3 · Then the genAI planning partner (~20 min)
  • In the BDA Planner, press Check this on each of the three panes — Before, During, After. Follow up with Ask me.
  • The genAI planning partner is a critic, not a designer. If it rewrites your lesson, push back and keep the parts that are you.
Wrap-up & looking ahead (~5 min):
  • Before next class:
    • Continue working on your lesson plan revisions.
    • Look over the Investigation Plan assignment. That's our lesson plan assignment.
  • Next class is revision day. Donuts are on me.
