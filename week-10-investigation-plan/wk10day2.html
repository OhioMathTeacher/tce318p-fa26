Week 10 · Second session — Revision day 🍩

Act II · revise the whole lesson · the last look before the field
Today's goals: Revise your Investigation Plan from both reads — your group's and the genAI planning partner's — and get ready to take it into the field. Donuts.

1 · The assignment and the field ahead (~20 min)
  • What you submit. Open the Investigation Plan assignment together. Ask your questions about the deliverable now.
  • What you do with it. In Weeks 11–12 you take this lesson into your placement and run it. Read the field-teaching guidelines together: you teach one or more of your pieces (Before, During, and/or After) — not necessarily the whole lesson — with one or more students, and you collect the work. Pseudonyms; a backup student on the same concept if yours is out.
  • Quick go-around: given what you know of your placement, which piece(s) are you likely to teach, and what will you be able to collect?

2 · Revise (~60 min)
  • Revise your plan in the BDA Planner from both reads: your peer-review sheets first, then the genAI planning partner's.
  • Start your reflection: which feedback did you act on, which did you set aside, and why?
  • Make your Investigation Plan. In the Planner, go to Share → Document 1. PDF is what you submit. Word is there if you want to keep editing in Word or Google Docs; save it as a PDF when you're done. On an iPad, the Word button takes two taps: the first builds the file, the second saves it.
Wrap-up & looking ahead:
  • The Investigation Plan is due Sunday.
  • Next: Weeks 11–12 — you take this lesson into the field, run one or more of its pieces, and collect your students' work. Everything you gather feeds your final report.
