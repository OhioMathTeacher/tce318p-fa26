2011 Mathematics · Grade 4 · Block M8 · Item 2011-4M8 · Constructed response
An amusement park has games, rides, and shows. The total number of games, rides, and shows is 70. There are 34 rides. There are two times as many games as shows. How many games are there? How many shows are there? Use numbers, words, or drawings to show how you got your answer.
Score levelDescription
Extended24 games and 12 shows, with correct explanation or work.
SatisfactorySubtraction error but games and shows in the correct 2 : 1 ratio; OR 12 games and 24 shows with work; OR 24 games and 12 shows with no work.
PartialFinds 36 and a 2 : 1 ratio (but not 24 : 12) with a sum under 36; OR 36 and 18; OR 72 and 36; OR a process that shows understanding but not the correct ratio.
MinimalFinds 36 by subtracting (or adding up to 70); OR games + shows = 36; OR a 2 : 1 ratio but nothing else correct.
IncorrectIncorrect response.

Source: NAEP Questions Tool, released item 2011-4M8 #19. Public domain. Holistic; built for this task only.
