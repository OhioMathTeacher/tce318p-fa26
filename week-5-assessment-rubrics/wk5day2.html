Week 5 · Second session — Clinic

Assessment · the field investigation, in one picture · Assessor · judge one learner, then meet the project it opens
Today's goals: First, do the Assessor's move on your own — read one real learner's work, name its DOK, and write the question you would ask next. Then open the field investigation: the back half of the course, built on one mathematical concept and the students learning it.

1 · Judge one learner — a Misconception Quick Response (~30 min)

  • Your second Misconception Quick Response — in class, on your own, no AI, collected today. Everything you need is in Pedagogical Excursion #5.

Purpose: last class you explored the lens with your table; now you point it yourself, at one real learner's work. That is the Assessor's move.

2 · Open the field investigation together (~45 min)

  • The back half of the course is one field investigation: one mathematical concept, and one or more students whose work shows how they understand it. Concept → question → design → collect → analyze → share.
  • The shape of it. On the course home page, Act II and Act III lay out every piece — what it is, what it's worth, and when it's due. We walk that table together.
  • Next week. We open the Concept Profile — your Week 6 field-dip assignment — and read it through. With your host teacher: choose one concept, find the student(s) whose work on it you'll follow, and bring back three to five pieces of their actual work.

Purpose: every role you built this block — learner, listener, poser, planner, assessor — was practice for aiming them at one concept and the real students learning it. This is where they meet.

If you finish the MQR early

  • Swap with a neighbor and read their questions. Which one best invites the student's own reasoning — without nudging them toward the answer? Say why.
