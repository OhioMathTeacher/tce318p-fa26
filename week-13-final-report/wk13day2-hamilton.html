Week 13 · Second session: Writing day · Hamilton

Act III · the week back · What did you seek to learn, and what did you learn?
Today's goals: Write your Collected Data while the field is fresh, and trade a paragraph with a classmate. Then find two or three sources that speak to what you saw.

1 · Write your Collected Data (~35 min)
  • Write your research question at the top of your draft.
  • Pick up from Writing Up Your Data. Put in your telling pieces as figures, label and caption each one, and write what each shows about how your student understood the concept.

2 · Trade a paragraph (~15 min)
  • Trade one Collected Data paragraph with a neighbor. The reader answers one question: can you see in the figure what the writer says is there?

3 · Find your research (~30 min)
  • Start with the sources in your Week 6 Concept Profile and the course readings.
  • Search JSTOR (log in through Miami's library) for your concept, in NCTM journals such as Teaching Children Mathematics.
  • Leave with two or three sources that speak to what you saw. Save the link or DOI for each one.
Wrap-up & over the break:
  • Over Thanksgiving break, we recommend a full rough draft of all five parts. Rough is fine.
  • Sign up with a partner for a 3-minute presentation at the Week 15 potluck: Presentations & Potluck sign-up.
  • Bring your draft to the first session of Week 15 (Oxford Mon Nov 30, Hamilton Tue Dec 1). A classmate reads it.
  • The Review & Reflection is due Sun Dec 6. The Final Report is due Wed Dec 9.
