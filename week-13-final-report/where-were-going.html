What's Todd-in-a-Can?

Todd-in-a-Can is a prompt, not a website. It is on the Review & Reflection guideline. Open your AI assistant (we recommend Gemini), paste the prompt, then paste your draft underneath it and send. It reviews your draft. It will not write your paper for you.
