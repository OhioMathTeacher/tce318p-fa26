Week 13 · First session: Welcome back

Act III · the week back · What did you seek to learn, and what did you learn?
Today's goals: You taught your lesson in the field and came back with student work. Tell us what you taught and what you collected. Then we go over the two assignments left, the Final Report and the Review & Reflection, and the plan from here to December 9.

1 · Field debrief (~45 min)
  • With a partner (15 min).
    • What did you teach: the Before, the During, the After? What did you collect, and what were you not able to get? What surprised you?
    • What's something funny that happened during the two weeks?
    • What's something that you learned about yourself as a teacher, your students as learners, and the mathematics as content you may teach?
  • With the room (30 min). Share your research question and one telling piece of student work or talk. The room tells you what they see in it.
  • Keep notes on what your colleagues say about your piece. You can use them in your Collected Data.

2 · What's left (~35 min)
  • The Final Report (50 points). Open the Final Report guidelines together. Ask your questions about the paper now.
  • The Review & Reflection (30 points). Open the Review & Reflection guidelines. It is a separate assignment, due Sun Dec 6.
  • The rest of the semester on one page: Where We're Going →
Wrap-up & tonight:
  • Pull your telling pieces. De-identify them, and keep the raw version of each for your appendix.
  • Start Writing Up Your Data tonight. It is not collected. It becomes your Collected Data.
  • Next class is a writing day. Bring your artifacts and the guidelines.
  • Sign up with a partner for a 3-minute presentation at the Week 15 potluck: Presentations & Potluck sign-up.
