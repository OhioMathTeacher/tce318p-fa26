Week 1 · Second session — Clinic

Operations & Algebraic Thinking · Learner · making your own thinking visible
Today's goals: Name what last class was — figuring things out by reasoning, where the struggle was the point — then play all three games in the app, watching your own thinking with playback and mark-up. At the end we'll look at your first Pedagogical Excursion together.

1 · The reading, and how we keep it (~25 min)

  • The article (~10). McFeetors & Palfy — what resonated? New ideas? Questions? Then back to the puzzles: how did you figure out the rules, where was the struggle? Name it — inductive reasoning; the struggle was the point; process over answer.
  • Some notes I took (~10). Journaler with the article open: a margin note, clicking it to jump back to the passage, then This Week's Entry — prompt, evidence, response.
  • Say it plainly: fine if you haven't started; the Journal isn't collected until after Week 5, and you can revisit any week.
  • You try it (~5). Mark one passage, write what you notice. No AI needed.

Purpose: discuss the reading, then show what keeping it looks like — a worked example beats an explanation.

Before class — loading my notes on this machine

  • Journaler → ⚙ → Backups → Restore… → todd-mcfeetors-notes.json.
  • Open the McFeetors PDF too — the backup has the notes, not the article, and the notes only appear once it's open.
  • Check: 15 notes, yellow on pp. 535–544, a response under This Week's Entry.

2 · Game 1 — Metadoku, together (~20 min)

  • Look first (~2). Open Metadoku. How is this different from games you usually meet? Which ideas from McFeetors show up here?
  • Play it together (~18): playback, mark-up, and the report you download.
  • The process is the same in all three, so this one is enough to work from.
  • Debrief: what did the playback show you that you missed while solving?

Purpose: read the app the way we read the article — the design is visible before you play, invisible after.

3 · Game 2 — Metaplete (time permitting)

  • Now on your own: play Metaplete, then play it back and mark up your solving.
  • Debrief: was your thinking different in this game than in Metadoku?

4 · Game 3 — Metagons (time permitting)

  • Same again with Metagons — play, play it back, mark it up.
  • Debrief: across all three games, where did you have a real strategy, and where were you guessing?

5 · Look at the assignment together (~25 min)

  • We'll open Pedagogical Excursion #1 and read through it together — that's the one place the details live.
  • Choose your board now, while the games are fresh — 4×4 minimum for Metadoku/Metaplete, square or pentagon for Metagons.
  • Walk the rubric (20 pts): board (3) · annotated decision points (6) · strategy named (5) · learner → teacher reflection (6). Annotation wants real moments, not "move 1, move 2."
  • Download the report from the game (.md or PDF, no names) → submit in Canvas, not Journaler.
  • You'll finish it at home and submit it by Sunday.

If you finish early

  • Look back at your three games — which one do you want to write up for the assignment, and why?
