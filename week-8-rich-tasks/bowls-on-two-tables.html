Bowls on Two Tables

Six bowls of cereal are placed on two tables. Use chips to show a way that six bowls might be placed on the two tables below. Find more ways. Record an equation for each way you find in the box at the bottom of this sheet. Finally, tell how many ways you think there are to place six bowls on two tables.
